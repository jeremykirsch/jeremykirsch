## How I Think About AI

AI should not become another destination employees have to visit.

The goal is to embed intelligence into the flow of work — where it can listen, retrieve, recommend, coach, act, and escalate within clearly defined human boundaries.

I focus on building systems that move organizations from:

**Human → AI-Assisted → Automated → Agentic**

while preserving judgment, governance, provenance, and measurable business outcomes.
